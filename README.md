Your Personal Shopper (YPS) is an international shopping and parcel forwarding platform that helps customers purchase products from online stores in different countries and receive them at their home addresses worldwide. The platform provides virtual shipping addresses, parcel management, international delivery, and online shipment tracking to simplify cross-border shopping.

I developed this web-based platform to support a seamless shopping and shipping experience, allowing customers to manage their purchases, monitor parcel progress, and access international shipping services through an online account.

Key Features & Functionality

- Virtual Shipping Addresses: Enables customers to receive local shipping addresses in supported countries for international online shopping.
- Customer Registration & Authentication: Provides account registration and login functionality for accessing personalized services.
- Customer Dashboard: Allows users to manage their accounts, shopping details, and parcel information.
- Shopping Details Management: Enables customers to purchase information, product values, retailer details, invoices, and tracking numbers.
- Parcel Management: Supports the processing and management of incoming parcels and shipment.

A comprehensive accommodation booking platform designed to help travelers discover and explore hotels, villas, and holiday apartments across the Greek mainland and islands.

I developed this project using CodeIgniter and MySQL, focusing on a structured, reliable, and user-friendly web application for accommodation listings and travel-related services.

Key Features:
Accommodation listings for hotels, villas, and holiday apartments
Property information and destination-based browsing
Database-driven accommodation management
Dynamic web pages and user-friendly navigation
Backend development using CodeIgniter jQuery , JS and MySQL
